Introduction

Welcome to Paste MD. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information that you may provide while using our application.

Information Collection

Our application is designed to work entirely client-side, meaning:

Data Storage

All data processing occurs locally within your web browser. The application may use local storage for:
